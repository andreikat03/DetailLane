# DetailLane

DetailLane is a lightweight management app built for auto detailing businesses.

It helps you:
- Create quotes quickly
- Manage appointments
- Track active and completed jobs
- Store customer information
- Monitor revenue and basic business statistics
- Use the app directly from your phone or computer

Your business data is stored locally on your device.

## Add DetailLane to your phone

### iPhone / iPad

1. Open the DetailLane link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

DetailLane will appear on your Home Screen and can be opened like a normal app.

### Android

1. Open the DetailLane link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu**.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm the installation.

You can then launch DetailLane directly from your Home Screen.
